ABOUT US

A Safe Place For Animals That Need Extra Care

Maasai Mara Special Needs Animals Sanctuary exists to care for animals with disabilities and provide them with safety, compassion and dignity.

We focus on daily care, rehabilitation, protection and improving the quality of life of vulnerable animals.
